Payment structures

Flexible terms for qualified transactions.

We evaluate the right structure for each counterparty, order size, and stage of the relationship.

  • Documentary letters of credit — at sight or deferred payment
  • Documentary collection — including cash against documents
  • Wire transfer — advance, deposit, or staged payment as agreed
  • Open-account terms — subject to credit approval and relationship history

Shipping structures

Commercial options built around the lane.

Orders can be structured around supplier capability and buyer needs for Egypt and regional destinations.

  • EXW / FCA — pickup or carrier handoff at the agreed origin
  • FOB — commonly from U.S. Gulf and other export gateways
  • CFR / CIF — delivered to the named destination port
  • Packaging — standard bags, bulk bags, or other agreed formats

Final grade availability, specifications, credit, payment terms, pricing, and Incoterms are confirmed for each transaction.
